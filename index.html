What is UniClean?

UniClean is a free, open environmental reporting platform. It gives every citizen a simple way to document pollution, illegal dumping, litter, and other environmental issues — and put them on a live public map that governments, municipalities, NGOs, and communities can act on.

No account. No app download. Just open, capture, and report.

How It Works

1
Open UniClean in your browser — no download needed.
2
Tap "Report an Issue" and grant camera and location access.
3
Capture the issue live with your device camera. GPS locks automatically.
4
Add category, description, and submit.
5
Your report goes live on the public map instantly. The community responds.

The Five Stages

  • 📍 Spotted — Issue captured and GPS-pinned on the public map
  • 🤝 Claimed — Individual, NGO, business or government publicly commits
  • 🔨 In Progress — Cleanup updates posted live with GPS evidence
  • ✅ Resolved — After-photos confirm cleanup. Record is permanent forever.
  • ⚠️ Unclaimed — Auto-escalated if no one acts within the set window

Who Built UniClean?

UniClean is a platform by RE-versal — an environmental innovation initiative committed to turning awareness into measurable, community-driven action. UniClean is RE-versal's flagship civic reporting platform, built for pilots with municipalities, schools, trade fairs, NGOs, and communities across Africa and beyond.
