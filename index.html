About UniClean

See it. Capture it. Resolve it.

Open UniClean, grant camera and location access, capture the issue live, and post it to the map. No account needed. UniClean is a community environmental reporting platform by RE-versal that creates real-time public accountability for pollution and environmental damage.

The Five Stages

  • 🐠 Spotted — Issue captured live on-camera and GPS-pinned publicly
  • 🤝 Claimed — Individual, NGO, business or government publicly commits to clean up
  • 🔨 In Progress — Live update photos posted. Community watches.
  • ✅ Resolved — After-photo evidence posted. Record is permanent.
  • ⚠️ Unclaimed — Auto-escalated to admin contact if no one acts in time

Built for pilots

Municipalities · Trade fairs · Schools · NGOs · Community campaigns. Share the QR code — anyone scans and reports in under 60 seconds. No login. No app.
